# Lesson 079 delivery design

The authorized curriculum unit is the Year 2 writing capstone: one-page regime → model decision guide. Keep the main route short, retrieval-first, and conditional on prediction-time information, split, budget, cardinality and text. Use explicit falsifiers rather than declare a universal model winner. No new architecture is introduced.

An essay alone matches the curriculum but underserves the requested reproducibility. A new all-family benchmark would change the unit and imply unsupported comparability. Chosen design: the essay plus a fully executable, source-visible audit of all corrected L060 v2 predictions; matched-cohort ranks explain why a benchmark headline cannot determine the deployment choice. A synthetic validation/latency intervention teaches constrained selection without reselecting on test scores.

Deliverables: HTML, printable reference/blank decision table, student/solution notebooks, portable figures, compressed hash-pinned complete evidence, source inventory, exact replay commands, rubric and model essay. Analysis replay is separate from training replay and published-paper reproduction. Reuse L060's audited metric/split/rank implementation inline. Full upstream training and paper benchmarks remain clearly unrun in this unit. Authorship does not imply learner completion.

Checks: complete 210-record coverage, score reconstruction, ID isolation, candidate selection, independent rank oracle, matched population, negative mutations, deterministic notebook replay, source parity, browser controls/mobile/print, copied Pages link delivery.
