# Lesson 097 design and delivery

The existing year-3 roadmap authorizes a Tier B negative-sampling training lesson. Keep the source and relation fixed while corrupting the typed destination. The learner should explain the negative-label assumption and identify whether a changed score comes from training or evaluation.

Considered scopes: a synthetic-only sampler exercise would expose the mechanism but omit the requested complete experiment; a historical BPR benchmark replay would introduce a separate task and protocol beyond this curriculum unit. The implemented scope combines a worked exact sampler trace with the full ML-100K release and a fixed-budget 45-fit course ablation. It retains explicit NOT_RUN/NOT_ESTABLISHED labels for historical BPR experiments.

Artifacts: canonical prose and HTML, whole-computation SVG/portable PNG, reusable distribution intervention, three live notebook TODOs with immediate checks, complete visible implementation, fresh executed solution, reference, source snapshots, protocol/deviation contract, per-user rankings, independent audit, manifest integration and copied-Pages/browser checks.

Execution: original full data, five official folds, three model seeds and three sampling arms. No data/epoch caps, hyperparameter search, early stopping, cached model reuse or opaque resume. Train-only exclusion and degrees; fixed full-catalog main metrics; same-score sampled-candidate diagnostic. Seed/fold variability remains descriptive.
