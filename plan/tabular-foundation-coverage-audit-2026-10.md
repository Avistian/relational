# Tabular foundation model coverage audit · 2026-10-02

**Scope:** calendar 2025 and 2026 through 2 October, including the trailing year (2025-10-02–2026-10-02). Older papers appear only to explain a missing family. This extends the [September influence audit](./research-influence-audit-2026-09.md) and updates the [bridge specifications](./year-5-6-bridge.md). It is a literature/teaching-plan revision, not model execution, lesson delivery or learner mastery.

## Search and evidence boundary

Compared the existing B01–B24 plan and curriculum registry against the [Mindful Modeler 2025 sitemap](https://mindfulmodeler.substack.com/sitemap/2025), [2026 sitemap](https://mindfulmodeler.substack.com/sitemap/2026), its linked papers and releases, the [LAMDA survey authors' maintained inventory](https://github.com/LAMDA-Tabular/Tabular-Survey), targeted arXiv searches, and the [ICML structured-data workshop programme](https://icml-structured-fm-workshop.github.io/accepted-papers/). Sitemaps exposed 18 and 25 post titles respectively; titles were screened, with relevant articles selectively read below. This is a documented coverage pass, **not a claim that every paper or every post was fully read**. Search indexing and accessible archive pages can omit material.

The [specific July 22 trends post requested by the user](https://mindfulmodeler.substack.com/p/trends-in-tabular-foundation-research) was read, including its workshop and two highlighted-paper links. An arXiv alternative recovered one paper after OpenReview blocked access. Its observation that the field is expanding into systems, new task types and richer evaluation motivates the additions. The programme is a discovery source; workshop acceptance alone does not establish influence or replication. Application-only papers and the whole time-series-FM literature are outside this relational thesis bridge.

Primary-source depth is marked below: **M** = inspected method/evaluation HTML; **A** = verified primary abstract/metadata, detailed code/protocol audit pending; **R** = official repository/model card/report; **P** = pointer only/access blocked. Blog recommendations are secondary orientation, never the evidence for a leaderboard winner. No new current SOTA claim is made.

Live-board access: [TabArena](https://tabarena.ai/) and [TabBench](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Neuralk-AI/tabbench) exposed application shells, not reproducibly extracted results. [RelBench's leaderboard](https://star-project.stanford.edu/relbench/leaderboard/) returned “No entries yet” in the accessible view; this does not establish that its underlying data are empty. Current rankings remain `NOT_CHECKED`. The [RelBench project](https://star-project.stanford.edu/relbench/) now documents newer tooling; historical course experiments must retain their original release/data identity.

## What was already covered

B03–B09 already cover TabPFN through 3.5, TabICLv2, TabDPT/Turbo, Mitra/v2, CARTE/ConTextTab/TabSTAR, LimiX variants, TabFM, EXAONE and Nori. B10–B14 cover RT, RelGNN/RelGT, Griffin/OpenRFM/KumoRFM, RDB-PFN/PluRel and flattening via RDBLearn/TabPFN-Rel. BeyondArena, temporal validity, contamination and relational context failures already have B18–B19. These are retained rather than counted as discoveries. This pass verifies gaps and selected updates; it does not repeat every September method audit.

### The July trends post's two highlighted papers

- [Beyond Accuracy: Toward Trustworthy Tabular Foundation Models in Industrial Applications](https://openreview.net/forum?id=r3RAi8Kqzl): **P / FULL_TEXT_NOT_CHECKED**. Relevant to B19's evaluation dimensions; OpenReview browser challenge prevented paper inspection.
- [Exploring Differences Between Tabular Enterprise Data and Public Benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.30452v1): **M**, recovered on arXiv after the [OpenReview link](https://openreview.net/forum?id=PXSBtjo3Gd) was blocked. Sections 3–5 compare a curated internal enterprise collection with public benchmarks and report differences in data composition and model rankings. B19 adds a representativeness audit: record semantic types, task selection and provider involvement. The internal corpus prevents assuming public reproducibility or population-wide prevalence.

The enterprise-data paper is now a B19 reading; Beyond Accuracy remains a follow-up pointer only. Neither is a required reproduction target. For the enterprise study, separate the public protocol from unavailable internal data; for Beyond Accuracy, check the paper before attributing substantive results.

## Teaching changes and limits

Add **B04a ◆** (scale axes), **B07a ◆** (hypernetworks), **B18a ★** (context as deployed model state), **B19a ★** (distributional evaluation), and **B19b ◆** (task expansion). Existing B01–B24 anchors remain valid; suffix units are taught immediately after their parent. Core becomes 18 units; electives become 11; total 29 planned units. Budget 80–126 core hours and 25–39 elective hours (105–165 total, about 15–24 weeks at one hour/day). Added hours include the strengthened checks and short linked readings; longer optional paper audits require an explicit extension.

The new core requirements concern evaluating/deploying a predictor, not reproducing every candidate. All proposed empirical exercises remain `NOT_RUN`; B23 retains the named-experiment approval and aggregate US$10 cap, including preparation, validation and retries. No paid runs were started. Full pretraining, independent benchmark confirmation, publication and learner mastery are not established by this revision.
