Full curriculum family inventory with dated source links, explicit inclusion/exclusion rationale and open/access boundaries. Worked proposal is an example, never a learner submission. Printable 3–5-page proposal template; B18a context/update/cost and B19a metric/calibration contracts; conditional forecasting extension. L201–204/L219 handoff.

Five rubric axes 0–2; eligibility requires >=8, no zero, resolved leakage and reproduction evidence appropriate to the declared claim. Assessment remains PENDING_WRITTEN_DEFENSE until teacher reads actual learner work. Interactive eligibility uses hypothetical evidence and never upgrades B23 historical status. Two preregistered tests require a named simpler-baseline comparison and untouched database/task, matched information/selection budgets, primary metric, numeric reversal rule and action.
