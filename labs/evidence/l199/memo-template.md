# My primary-direction memo

This is a draft for review, not a registered study or permission to launch.

- Direction: choose one hypothesis to investigate; state whether this is provisional.
- Hypothesis: what result would challenge the claim?
- Contrast: precise arms, shared information contract, and estimand.
- Baselines: task, healthy implementation, validation-only choices, untouched evaluation.
- Metric: direction, units and reporting scope.
- Threshold: justified useful-effect margin fixed before new outcome inspection.
- Uncertainty: sampling unit, seeds, dependence and inconclusive region.
- Cost: all preparation, seeds, retries, validation, overhead and stop reserve.
- Stop: mandatory data/source/design/cost gates and observable kill condition.
- Deferred: strongest alternative, its opportunity cost and reopening trigger.
- Evidence: source/version/identity, what was actually replayed, what remains unknown.
- Revision: result or new information that would change this choice.

Write 400–700 words. Then send the memo to the agent for review. Automated field checks do not establish scientific quality or learner mastery.
