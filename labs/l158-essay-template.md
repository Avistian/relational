## 1. Bounded verdict
State the claim, population and current conclusion. Separate predictive quality, human effort and broad undervaluation.

## 2. Evidence for
Cite the published source separately from local execution. Identify task, metric, split, seed set and comparator.

## 3. Evidence against and missing
Use the matched F1 comparison, its magnitude and conditional interval. Include missing recommendation test, human effort and graph-construction evidence.

## 4. Validity and dependence
Explain released versus fixed-horizon policy; retain unknown historical availability and gradient limitations. Trace one reused prediction packet. Distinguish seed variability from new-database uncertainty.

## 5. What would change my mind?
Specify task/database, comparator, legal information, useful threshold, validation selection, held-out decision, uncertainty method, seeds, fair budget and prospective effort observations. Explain a disconfirming outcome and an inconclusive outcome.

## Review rubric
0 = absent/incorrect; 1 = partial; 2 = accurate and justified. Numerical/provenance accuracy; comparator fairness; uncertainty/dependence; counter-evidence; falsifiability. Target 8/10 with no zero, after teacher review. Passing code checks alone does not pass the essay.
