TabM: operator and protocol

Corrected v2 numeric mini: train-only median/z-score → [B,k,p] views → learned R₁ initialized ±1 → N shared linear weights AND biases, ReLU/dropout → k independent heads → mean scalar predictions or mean softmax probabilities. Mini has no S, later R, or member backbone bias. Full TabM retains R,S and member biases in each block, with later multiplicative adapters initialized to 1 and identical random bias rows.

Decisions that change the experiment

DecisionPrecise distinction
Independent versus packed ensembleSame unshared backbone architecture; independent MLP×k selects each member separately, packed selects/tunes the collective predictor.
Train versus inferMean of member supervised losses during training; mean predictions during inference. Neither pooling hidden vectors nor averaging logits implements this classification rule.
Shared versus independent batchesSmall suite broadcasts B rows and targets; closer procedure gives each member its own B-row shuffle with matched [B,k] labels.
Rank-one modulationdiag(r)Wdiag(s), usually full-rank when W is. Not an additive low-rank update; signs are only initialization.
Convexity versus learned advantageEnsemble MSE/RMSE and cross-entropy cannot exceed mean member values. Classification error can: positive-class probabilities .51,.51,.01 give 1/3 individual error and 1 collective error.

TabM operator and objective card¶

Row-vector layer: ((x ⊙ Rᵢ)W) ⊙ Sᵢ+bᵢ. With x=(2,3), W=[[1,2],[3,4]], R=(1,1) gives (11,16); R=(1,−1) gives (−7,−8), before activation.
