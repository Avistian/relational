TabM: parameter-efficient ensembling

One MLP, k predictions. TabM is a deep ensemble of k MLPs that share almost all weights and are trained jointly; the prediction is the mean of the k submodels. Default k = 32, the only new hyperparameter over a plain MLP.

BatchEnsemble linear layer (§3.2)

Shared W; per-member multiplicative adapters rᵢ (input), sᵢ (output) and additive bᵢ:

ObjectRule
Member layerlᵢ(x) = sᵢ ⊙ (W (rᵢ ⊙ x)) + bᵢ
Equivalent matrixWᵢ = W ⊙ (sᵢ rᵢᵀ) (rank-one sign mask on shared W)
Batched over kl_BE(X) = ((X ⊙ R) W) ⊙ S + B, X ∈ ℝ[k,d]
Overhead3d numbers per member per layer (vs d² for W)

Forward pass and training

Numeric input → z-score → broadcast to k copies [B,k,p] → first ±1 adapter R₁ diversifies copies → N shared blocks Linear(W)/ReLU/Dropout → k packed heads → mean over k (softmax probabilities for classification, raw for regression). Objective = mean over members of each member's loss; inference = mean of member predictions; stop on the ensemble's validation score. Init: non-first R,S = 1 (no-op), first R = random ±1, B = 0.

Why it works (§3.5, §5)

Weight sharing regularises (fewer dead neurons: 0.14 at k=32 vs 0.29 at k=1). Joint training enables ensemble-aware early stopping and single-model tuning. Submodels are weak and overfit individually but strong collectively — direct evidence of diversity; even the best submodel ≈ a plain MLP. Averaging variance: σ²(ρ + (1−ρ)/k), floor √ρ·σ — diversity (low ρ) matters more than large k. Too-high k can hurt; narrow/shallow TabM is suboptimal. Submodels are prunable (≈8.8/32 kept greedily).

Interpretation checks

A member is not a new matrix — it is the shared W with a rank-one ±1 mask. "Default k=32" is not tuned. Collective > individual is the transferable claim; a win over trees is regime-dependent. A capped, width-64, numeric run is INCOMPARABLE to the 46-dataset benchmark (no feature embeddings, no domain-aware splits, no per-model tuning). p>0.05 on three datasets is low power, not equivalence.

Local evidence (this lab)

Three reused TabR-release splits, caps 1200/600/600, three seeds, TabM-mini width 64 / depth 3 / 64 epochs / k=32, deep ensemble MLP×32, six-candidate XGB. Diversity effect (collective < mean individual) reproduces on all three, and MLP×32 beats a single MLP on all three. Head-to-head is mixed: TabM-mini beats the single MLP on House and Higgs (loses on California) and beats MLP×32 only on Higgs; XGB wins California and Higgs but is last on House. Mean ranks tie MLP×32 and XGB (2.0), then TabM-mini (2.67), then MLP (3.33); Friedman p=0.53. Verdict INCOMPARABLE. See the reproducibility contract and local evidence.

TabM operator and objective card¶

Row-vector layer: ((x ⊙ Rᵢ)W) ⊙ Sᵢ+bᵢ. With x=(2,3), W=[[1,2],[3,4]], R=(1,1) gives (11,16); R=(1,−1) gives (−7,−8), before activation.

Decision Local computation Common substitution to catch
Train Mean of member losses Loss of mean probability
Classify Mean of softmax probabilities Softmax of mean logits
Share One W, member adapters/biases/heads k independent dense matrices
Prune Subset of one trained model Claiming this equals retraining at smaller k

Equal-variance intuition: Var(mean error)=σ²[ρ+(1−ρ)/k]. Correlated error remains even with many members. State mini/full variant and measure activation memory and runtime alongside parameters.
