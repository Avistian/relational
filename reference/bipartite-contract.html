Bipartite recommendation: the contract

Turn an interaction table into an auditable prediction task before choosing a neural model.

ObjectDefinition / check
Typed identityuser:0 and item:0 are distinct. Every original graph edge crosses types.
Rating versus likeObserved ratings are events; in L095, ratings ≥4 create likes. A low rating is observed but not a like.
Forward/reverse viewsBuild both from fitting rows; compare sets after reversing coordinates. Held-out pairs must appear in neither.
BBinary user×item fitting likes. BBᵀ counts shared items; BᵀB counts shared users. Projection loses intermediate identity.
WalkP=row_normalize(B), Q=row_normalize(Bᵀ), S=PQP. User→item→user→item. Fitting popularity handles zero-degree users.
Candidate maskExclude all fitting-rated items, not just liked ones. Unknown is not the same as disliked.
Recall@KRelevant top-K hits divided by total held-out relevant items for the user.
NDCG@KSum relevance/log₂(rank+1); divide by the ideal sum. Binary relevance in this lesson.
Macro denominatorAverage users with at least one held-out relevant item; report excluded users.
Selection boundaryChoose α on inner validation, refit official base, then evaluate official test. Test scores never select α.
Time boundaryOffline random completion is not future-event prediction. A forecasting claim needs a cutoff and availability rules.
Reproduction boundaryRelease counts/partitions MATCH. Full course run MEASURED. No historical model score claimed.

Trace without looking

u0 likes i0,i1; u1 likes i1,i2. The u0→i1→u1→i2 path has probability ½×½×½=⅛. The full u0 endpoint row is [⅜,½,⅛]. Mask i0,i1 to recommend i2. Remove u1–i1 and the probability of i2 becomes zero.
