Beta–Bernoulli PFN card¶

Prior Beta(α,β) × likelihood θˢ(1−θ)ᶠ gives posterior Beta(α+s,β+f). Next-success probability is (α+s)/(α+β+s+f). Under Beta(1,1), s=3,f=1 gives 2/3.
