Measured evidence and limit

Across three trained seeds, mean absolute posterior error averages 0.0259 on the checked in-range grids and 0.0429 at unseen context length 64. The plot shows each seed's range around its mean. The learned approximation is useful but does not inherit the analytic formula's exact extrapolation.
