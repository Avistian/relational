The three computations

Dense: x′ = x + x₀ ⊙ (Wx + b)
Linear factors: x′ = x + x₀ ⊙ [U(Vᵀx) + b]
Experts: x′ = x + Σₖ gₖ(x) Eₖ(x)

x₀ is the original embedded row; x is the current layer state. W is [d,d], U and V are [d,r], b is [d]. The bias lies inside x₀ multiplication. With row batches [B,d] and PyTorch [out,in] weights, use XWᵀ. No other rows enter the forward path. Paper §3, Eqs.1–4.

Fast hand check

Take x₀=x=[2,3], W=[[1,1],[0,1]], b=[1,−1]. Affine mixture [6,2] → crossed update [12,6] → residual result [14,9]. Two scalar layers with x=2, every w=1 and every b=0 give 18. The wrong current-input anchor gives 42. A non-symmetric W catches a missing transpose.

What is verified?

Mathematics / implementation: independent coordinate sums, finite-difference gradient checks, exact factorization, the actual pinned TFRS Cross.call method executed with torch-backed Dense adapters, anchored degree examples, nonlinear expert checks, both layouts and cross-weight gradient flow. This is not a TensorFlow-runtime or optimizer-parity test. Source choices and hashes.

Local training: credit_g, diabetes, blood_transfusion × seeds 0/1/2, fixed stratified 65/15/20 split seed 5. Train-only vocabularies and scaling. Neural arms: 20 epochs, Adam .001, batch 64, embedding 4, hidden [32,32], two crosses, rank min(4,max(1,d//4)). CatBoost: 300 iterations, depth 6, LR .05. Select validation log loss; summarize test AUROC ranks. Unequal capacities and initializations; no tuning search.

Full MovieLens closer run: 739,012 filtered rows, three seeds, five epochs. Log loss 0.355545 ± 0.000692; AUROC 0.864981 ± 0.000413; sample SD across seeds. About 204 seconds CPU training. MovieLens task and provenance

GroupLens MovieLens 1M: remove rating 3; map 1/2→0 and 4/5→1. Random 80/10/10 split, seed 48. Inputs: user, movie, gender, age, occupation, ZIP; vocabularies fitted on training rows; missing/unseen code 0. The precise six-field mapping and original split indices are not provided by the inspected paper. No genres, labels or timestamps enter forward.

Do not compare the TFRS tutorial’s raw-rating RMSE with this binary log-loss task. Randomly held-out interactions are not cold-start or temporal evaluation. Archive MD5 from GroupLens: c4d9eecfca2ab87c1945afe126590906. The loader verifies this and records SHA-256. The author's host certificate had expired; the public archive was downloaded with an explicit temporary TLS bypass and checked against the publisher's checksum. The reusable loader retains TLS verification; an existing verified archive can be supplied if needed. MD5 here checks archive identity, not resistance to an adversarial forgery.

Read the ledger literally

NOT_RUN: no corresponding run. INCOMPARABLE: protocol differs; even a coincidentally close score cannot validate the original result. MATCH / CLOSE / FAIL: permitted only after protocol agreement, using a declared tolerance. This runner always records the outstanding deviations and returns INCOMPARABLE. It cannot automatically certify them away.
