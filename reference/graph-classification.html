Arithmetic to retrieve

Center [1,0], neighbors [1,0] and [0,1]: GIN-0 MLP input [2,1]. Sum of {A,A,B} with one-hot encodings: [2,1]; mean [2/3,1/3]; max [1,1]. Raw scalar sums are not injective: {1,3} and {2,2} collide.

h_v' = MLP((1 + epsilon) h_v + sum_{u in N(v)} h_u)
z_G  = sum_{k=0}^4 head_k(sum_{v in G} h_v^k)

Release: four updates; each MLP has two affine maps and internal BatchNorm/ReLU, plus outer BatchNorm/ReLU. Heads read the input and every depth. Dropout applies to each head's logits during training. GIN-0 fixes epsilon to zero.

WL boundary

Update every node from its previous color and multiset of previous neighbor colors, with a joint dictionary across graphs. Different graph color histograms certify non-isomorphism. Equal histograms are inconclusive. A six-cycle and two triangles remain indistinguishable with identical initial labels. Theoretical injectivity conditions are not a guarantee about learned finite networks.

Selection boundary

For each configuration, average ten validation curves before choosing a common epoch. Then choose the configuration. Report fold variability at that epoch. No untouched test set is present. Separate maxima per fold implement a different selection rule.
