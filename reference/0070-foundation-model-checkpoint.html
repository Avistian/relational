Final checkpoint decision card¶

Identity: dataset/split IDs, class mapping, checkpoint revision/hash, inference recipe and seed role. Evidence: saved predictions, reconstructible validation selection, complete roster and failure status. Decision: task-level effects, dataset ranks, conditional uncertainty and lifecycle cost.

Seven arms × five tasks × three seeds = 105 selected results. Adding arms can change ranks while old predictions and losses stay fixed. Five datasets remain five task blocks.
