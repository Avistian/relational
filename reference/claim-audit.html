Make the claim fit the experiment

One-sentence contract: Model variant, on dataset version and population, under split and available information, with selection budget, achieves metric and uncertainty relative to specified baselines.

Three distinct evidence levels

EvidencePermitted statement
Paper readingThe authors report this result in this version and table.
Copied-weight validationThese implementations agree on the checked inputs, outputs, gradients and variant.
Experiment replayThis code/configuration produced these scores on these partitions.

MATCH/CLOSE/FAIL need a comparable target and a declared tolerance. INCOMPARABLE means essential protocol differences prevent that verdict; NOT_RUN means the experiment has not been executed. Numerical proximity cannot remove a protocol mismatch.

Mechanism reminders

OperationRule and trap
ExcelFormer SPAAttention rows receive; columns send. With descending feature importance, allow j≤i. Blocking weaker→stronger attention does not remove the weaker token from the final pooling head.
MI orderingFit using training rows and labels only. Stable ordering breaks equal-MI ties positionally in released code; paper Eq.2 allows equal-MI communication both ways.
Feat-Mixλ = retained MI / total MI. Mix donor labels by λ. If total MI is zero, declare a fallback. Train-only augmentation is absent at inference.
Trompt Eq.4[B,P,d] @ [d,C] → [B,P,C]; softmax over C. The equation has no Transformer √d divisor.
Trompt Eq.5Multiply [B,P,C,1] weights by [B,P,C,d] expanded features; sum C → [B,P,d]. Downstream prompt pooling normalizes P, a different axis.

Before accepting “beats trees”

  1. Pin the paper version, table row, model and augmentation variant.
  2. Check dataset provenance, duplicated tasks, preprocessing fit scope, splits and prediction-time availability.
  3. Count unique fitted configurations, not duplicated trace entries. Record trials, compute, early stopping and checkpoint-selection metric.
  4. Separate AUROC, accuracy and log loss. Retain per-seed results and state what the intervals measure.
  5. Use distinct datasets for aggregate ranks. A nonsignificant rank test does not establish equivalence.
  6. Keep temporal transfer separate from IID reproduction, and unrun scale-up separate from measured evidence.
