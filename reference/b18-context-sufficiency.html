Question Rule
Which events count? Filter event and availability time by the query cutoff before counting or aggregating
What is degree? Number of eligible related events; declare the event types and time window
What is truncated? Raw sampled sum retains only the observed mass
When is N/k correction unbiased? Uniform sampling without replacement, exact eligible N, k>0
Does unbiased mean exact? No; concentrate most value in one event to expose variance
What is preserved by monthly sums? The historical sum query; not arbitrary ordering, extremes or future targets
What information does aggregation cost? Access to the complete eligible history, possibly maintained incrementally
Which bins are legal inference inputs? Bins defined from available history, never post-cutoff outcomes

For fixed historical sum S, E[Sₖ]=(k/N)S. Therefore E[(N/k)Sₖ]=S. Four events [10,20,30,40], k=2: corrected totals [60,80,100,100,120,140], mean 100. For a finite population with variance σ²=mean((x−mean(x))²), Var(Ŝ)=N²(1−k/N)σ²/k × N/(N−1), for N>1. At k=N variance is zero; concentrated amounts increase it.

Bias=mean(prediction−target); RMSE=sqrt(mean((prediction−target)²)). Report both, by historical degree, with every seed. In B18: 24 histories, 96 conditions, 100 draws each, four arms. This is historical reconstruction, not a forecast or a trained-model benchmark.
