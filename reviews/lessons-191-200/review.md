## Overall sequence

Published comparison → input admission → complete task accounting → report → challenge the thesis → ask a precise question → synthesize the landscape → formulate proposals → select a direction → defend evidence and a future experiment.

The core explanations are suitable for a returning Year 5 learner: key terms are reintroduced and each lesson has concrete arithmetic or a worked decision trace. Detailed tables and source audits are reference material alongside the short core. Architecture figures are appropriate in model lessons; experimental and evidence diagrams serve the planning lessons. L196 needs no neural architecture.

Validation receipts in this directory record actual checks. Publication, live Colab, fresh training, historical reproduction, novelty, community participation and learner mastery remain separate claims.
