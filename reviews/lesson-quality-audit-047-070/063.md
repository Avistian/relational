## Remaining limits and final status

The lab uses a bounded numerical subfamily: Gaussian roots instead of mixed roots, capped graph sizes, uniform choices rather than the complete hierarchical choice machinery, omitted categorical/missingness/feature-scale/full-wrapper preprocessing, and a deliberate overlap rejection. Thus **complete historical prior sampling is not reproduced**. Source conditional forward parity and inner rank parity do not establish RNG/training/distribution parity.

The measured predictor is an ordinary fixed logistic diagnostic. No PFN is pretrained here; no pretrained checkpoint is run. The exact Bayesian oracle covers two declared worlds only. **Original Table4/final benchmark results are not reproduced**, full prior fitting remains **NOT_RUN**, and the broader closer gate remains **NOT_RUN** by default.
