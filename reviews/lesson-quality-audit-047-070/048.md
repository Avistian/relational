## Verdict before and after

Before: the core implementation was faithful to the stated dense/linear-factor scope, with unusually clear degree-bound and INCOMPARABLE qualifications. The existing broader depth pass had already added recurrence, gradient and low-rank explanations. However, the unit did not sufficiently explain **what V2 changes over original DCN**, how prediction-level polynomial coefficients arise from weights plus the readout, or what the paper's different experiments isolate. A learner could execute the layer without understanding the paper's main expressiveness motivation. The package checker had also become stale after the newer architecture figure replaced its original overview, and the live-browser review exposed a clipped, undersized critical-difference diagram.

After: repaired teaching and runnable checks connect original-DCN restrictions, V2 coordinate mixtures, readout coefficients, exact construction of the paper's synthetic target, and the evidence from synthetic versus recommendation experiments. The model and trainer did not require a behavioral repair. Both notebooks, prepared HTML, lesson, canonical depth and reference are aligned. The full local experiment was reexecuted; all 36 AUROC values match the recorded reference exactly. All three full MovieLens saved test-prediction arrays reconstruct exactly from their selected checkpoints. These results validate this package's evidence, **not the paper's training results**.

The teaching comparison used L042's baseline/evidence separation, L043's operation-by-operation definitions and visible architecture, L044's numerical path trace, and L045's standalone task guidance and source/model separation. Their older empirical phrasing was not adopted as authority; equal widths are not equal model capacity or a causal isolation of one mechanism.
