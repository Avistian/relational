# Lesson062 audit and repair · historical TabPFN v1

## Verdict before and after

**Before:** the core explanation and lab treated a reduced randomly initialized two-layer row encoder with an unknown-label embedding as the learner implementation. Two attention/mask TODOs exercised that encoder, while actual historical pretrained predictions were produced by a separate packaged runner. The evidence was real but the learner's mechanism did not produce it. Important released mechanics—full12-layer postnorm architecture, linear label encoder without unknown-label token, source feature scaling/preprocessing, temperature and class-aligned logit averaging—were absent or underspecified. The older depth prose carefully qualified some limits but did not repair the disconnect.

**After:** the complete numeric historical inference mechanism is visible and versioned in `labs/relkit/tabpfn_l062_v2.py`. Five live functions feed the actual12-layer/512-width/4-head/1024-FFN/10-output model with copied original pretrained weights. The standalone lesson/lab derives the task objective, explains the SCM/BNN prior and its evidence limits, traces the mask change from061, tokenization, postnorm residuals, context-fitted transforms,100/k scaling, view generation, class inversion and logit reduction. A full-data three-paper-dataset experiment and actual teacher inference now run through that mechanism. Full original prior training and paper benchmark results are **not reproduced**.

A substantive additional finding overturns a tempting whole-wrapper invariance claim: an extreme added query can trigger the historical power-transform fallback for an entire feature column and change an existing prediction, despite context-only parameter fitting. This is measured against the released source and recreated with the learner's live functions. The operator is preserved; the explanation now distinguishes Transformer isolation, ordinary wrapper checks and the failure-path counterexample.

No mission/mastery changes or commits/pushes are made by the lesson author. Parent owns shared routes, glossary/retrieval, delivery and publication. Historical operators and evidence remain unchanged.
