# Lesson 054 audit and repair — TabM

## Verdict

**Before:** substantial-looking package, but not a faithful implementation of the advertised TabM-mini. The layer, architecture narrative, captions and evidence labels disagreed. The old implementation retained first-layer S and member backbone biases that mini removes; its transposed/3-D Kaiming initialization used incorrect fan-in. It also called independently checkpointed deep ensembles equivalent to TabM-packed, presented a class-error ordering as a required invariant, and inferred shared bias from a single fixed-rate k curve. The notebook lacked an executable closer procedure and a source parity check.

**After:** the lesson teaches a corrected, visible numeric mini architecture, with complete copied-weight forward/backward reference checks, a separately rerun fixed-budget evidence track, a fully executed live-function notebook, and a separate closer training procedure. Paper claims, architectural checks, local measurements and unrun work are now distinguished by exact operator identities. The lesson now explains selection, batch layouts, objective arithmetic, pruning, utilization and evaluation protocols rather than importing their conclusions from a short paper summary.

**Paper results reproduced: NO.** Numeric mini/full operators are checked; the capped four-arm suite and bounded closer smoke remain INCOMPARABLE to the tuned 46-task benchmark. Full-sized closer/paper presets, cloud runs and live Colab UI are not run. Parent owns browser/Pages/publication evidence and the separate commit/push.
