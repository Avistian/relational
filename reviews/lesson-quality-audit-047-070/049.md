# Lesson 049 — ExcelFormer and Trompt

## Verdict and concrete changes

Before repair, ExcelFormer had a useful visible numeric model, exact copied-weight results on a bounded evaluation fixture, three author-split tasks, a separate temporal transfer probe and an explicitly incomparable larger Pima attempt. Trompt had only two tensor primitives: neither its prompt/state fusion, learned value expansion, repeated cells nor shared supervised head could be trained. The notebook therefore did not teach one of the two named models end to end. The architecture figure candidly disclosed that omission, but disclosure alone did not close the teaching gap.

After repair, `labs/relkit/trompt_l049.py` provides a complete **numeric** Trompt mirror: per-cell numeric encoders, LayerNorm-normalized prompt/column identities, residual prompt fusion, column softmax, learned scalar-to-prompt expansion with ReLU/GroupNorm/residual, column reduction, repeated cells receiving the original input, shared prompt-pooling head, summed per-cell cross-entropy, and declared mean-logit inference. The lab exposes all these classes and the train/predict loop, adds a live loss TODO, instruments the actual student routing calls during training, checks gradients/recurrence/row independence, and puts a new, separately measured Pima execution probe in EXIT. The diagram now traces the original input into both cells, recurrent state, and shared head. Its portable PNG is regenerated from that responsive HTML.

The prose explains all normalization axes, both softmax axes, why the first cell cannot have row-specific routing, how learned expansion differs from broadcast, deep supervision versus loss after averaging, and why routing weights do not identify causal importance. It interprets Trompt's prompt/expansion/cell-count ablations and their changed regression target normalization. ExcelFormer gains precise source-level augmentation/training differences, finite-mask scope, rank-denominator cautions, and a concrete comparison with FT-Transformer, SAINT and DCNv2. Existing correct explanations, including .01 weight scaling versus 1e-4 variance scaling, are preserved.

**Neither paper's benchmark results have been reproduced.** The important improvement is a trained, inspectable Trompt mechanism and substantially stronger explanation, not a promoted reproduction label. The two old ExcelFormer experiment arms and their canonical model file are unchanged. New Trompt evidence is not inserted into their comparative rank statistics.
