## Concrete measured findings

The correction trained 27 TabM fold models in 408.6 seconds on a busy shared CPU
host. This excludes archived arm training and is not efficiency evidence. The main
library keeps the same three datasets, row cap750, split/fold seed57, three model
seeds, three folds and fixed hyperparameters. It is a **hybrid prediction archive**,
not 81 fresh fits.

Mean stack-minus-OOF-single gaps and paired training-seed 95% intervals:

| Dataset | Mean gap | Interval |
|---|---:|---|
| diabetes | −0.002743 | [−0.007487,+0.002001] |
| blood_transfusion | −0.000716 | [−0.002257,+0.000825] |
| phoneme | −0.023728 | [−0.030679,−0.016778] |

The first two intervals cross zero. Uniform weights beat learned weights on the
first two tasks. The learned stack does not beat the best individual *test* score
on any task; those test winners remain descriptive oracle choices.

TabM's mean blood-transfusion weight is 0.932714, but removing it and reselecting
improves test loss by 0.004016. Removing TabICL on phoneme worsens loss by 0.028266.
These are conditional, exploratory library diagnoses, not causal attributions.

Pinned upstream comparison: 4/9 exact matches; remaining maximum probability
change 0.003730906 and maximum OOF loss difference 0.000000743412. A controlled
reference modification disabling only score rounding yields 9/9 exact weights.
That diagnosis is explicitly separate from unmodified-source parity.
