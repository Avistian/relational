# Lesson 052 audit and repair — TabR

Audit date: 2026-09-10. Scope: only Lesson 052 and its canonical package. No learner/mastery records, shared mission, historical training code, commits, or publication operations changed by this lesson agent.

## Verdict

**Before:** the numeric TabR-S mechanism was substantially correct and independently readable, with four live implementation tasks, original author split membership, useful mechanism widgets, and honest INCOMPARABLE local evidence. The weaknesses were depth and precision: the design-step evidence was compressed; the regression correction analysis and scaling/update experiments were mostly named rather than explained; Trompt was absent from the bigger-picture comparison; the entire preprocessing/training/comparison implementation appeared in one 170-line cell; EXIT asked for a prediction trace without producing an inspectable real-row trace; a tied-neighbor CHECK assumed an ordering unsupported by Torch; and the `paper` preset wording overstated transfer of the released selected evaluation procedure.

**After:** repaired as a strong standalone **numeric TabR-S teaching and mechanism-validation package**, with explicit paper-study interpretation, a vector projection derivation, distinctions between kinds of interventions, concrete memory/runtime diagnosis, a verified live real-row prediction trace, annotated training stages, stronger source derivative validation, and an actionable official reference-evaluation lane. **Paper results are not reproduced.** The legacy `paper` preset remains a resource/settings preset with enumerated deviations, not a fidelity certificate. No new claim of full TabR, Faiss performance, stochastic training, ensembles, broad benchmark, temporal deployment, or cloud reproduction is made.
