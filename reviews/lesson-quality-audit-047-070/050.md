## Remaining limitations

This is a rigorous local comparison checkpoint, not a full study reproduction. It still lacks the released paper splits/preprocessing, the tuned search space, eight-head benchmark configurations, 15-seed evaluation, five-model ensemble groups, complete task suite, categorical/multiclass/regression branches and ResNet in its local three-arm race. The scale-up is an honest executable extension but preserves those mismatches. Exact parity checks a narrow deterministic model function. Three convenience datasets and three seeds on one split cannot support population-wide superiority or equivalence. The post-hoc ensemble diagnostic cannot provide a new untouched test estimate.
