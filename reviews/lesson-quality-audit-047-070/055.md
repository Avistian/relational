## Verdict before and after

It gave little account of feature engineering, the actual four-arm temporal experiment, original training/search rules, or the relationship among seeds, windows and dataset units. It retains the historical measured operators/results without changes and identifies all remaining fidelity gaps. It does **not** claim independent reproduction of paper training or full Figure 2.

Read in full: MISSION, relevant NOTES preferences, teach/pedagogy/lab-authoring/lesson-visuals instructions, existing055 HTML/depth/builder/model/trainer/checkers/contracts and representative042–045 lesson/lab sections.
