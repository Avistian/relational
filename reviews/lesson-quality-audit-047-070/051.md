## Before and after

The existing lesson already had unusually good intervention arithmetic: train-only smoothing, a matched top-five baseline, transported neural weights, independent noise generation, live student functions, honest local statistics, and six portable figures. It was stronger than a generic tabular-model overview. The audit nevertheless found substantive teaching and reproducibility gaps compared with the annotated mechanisms and tasks in 042–045:

1. **The illustrative smoothing story could teach the wrong empirical inference.** It described neural improvement while the paper's small-bandwidth finding concerns lost tree accuracy against the original evaluation task. Replaced it with a worked difference-of-differences example in which trees lose five points and the neural score stays fixed; derived why removal of useful training detail can produce this pattern. The numbers are explicitly illustrative.
2. **Paper protocol deviations were named but insufficiently reconstructed.** Added a full paper/local map: early-stopping validation versus hyperparameter-selection validation; nominal split and training cap; folds; training budget/patience; search and repeated orders; figure-specific score scales and uncertainty. A three-candidate search trace shows validation improving while selected test accuracy drops.
3. **One smoothing bandwidth could not diagnose manipulation strength.** Added a visible, executable four-bandwidth electricity diagnostic, preserving the historical model and experiment implementation. It measures label changes, class balance, selected checkpoints, raw-test probabilities and paired effects. It explicitly reports one-class target collapse without invented scores. This is a real-data extension of the paper's curve-shaped question, not another disclaimer.
4. **Smoothing edge cases and code/paper correspondence needed depth.** Added the covariance eigendirection derivation, Gaussian normalization cancellation, `cov_mult=h²`, numerical-floor meaning, the upstream robust fallback discrepancy, global-mean endpoint and duplicate-conflicting-row discontinuity at the h=0 identity branch. Extended numerical source parity from empirical covariance to a coupled-seed, well-conditioned MinCovDet case.
5. **Feature removal and architectural implications were too compressed.** Added retained-versus-discarded feature reasoning, duplicate-feature redundancy, XOR/zero-correlation counterexamples, a tokenization derivation showing why FT-T does not inherit the MLP weight-transport guarantee, and concrete comparisons with coordinate-based cross layers, selection masks and future retrieval. These are mechanism arguments and hypotheses, not new benchmark claims.
6. **Notebook and mobile delivery had fixable gaps.** Added a pre-setup standalone recap, explicit Goal/Why/Hint boundaries, duplicate-row CHECKs, the live diagnostic and its EXIT artifact/interpretation. Added a seventh portable figure, repaired CD styling/focus/scroll guidance, and made the new measured table horizontally scrollable with unsplit numeric cells and a full-size chart link. The root's browser inspection caught both the CD clipping and the initial diagnostic table wrapping.

No new model is introduced: the correct overview is an intervention/evaluation protocol. The existing familiar MLP and complete numeric FT-T implementation remains split into visible, annotated notebook cells. The protocol source now traces 1800 rows → 1080/360/360 split → d/k/3d feature conditions → training/validation selection → 360 raw-test probabilities.
