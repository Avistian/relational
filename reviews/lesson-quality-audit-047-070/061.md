## Measurements and interpretation

The author run fits three seeds for2,000updates each on the fixed-GP prior. At matched context16, PFN NLL is **−1.0189**, exact GP **−3.0532**, and ignoring context **1.4258**. Excess NLL is **2.0342 nats/query**. The model therefore uses context but remains far from the exact PPD. Central95% coverage is100% here, compatible with overly broad predictions rather than perfect uncertainty learning.

With the same frozen model and true task length scale changed to.1, context16 PFN NLL is **4.4927**, exact GP **−1.8757**, and coverage **39.3%**. Prior mismatch sharply damages this learned inference procedure. No claim is made about all priors or real datasets.

At matched context16, analytic GP region masses in the identical fixed head give NLL **−2.2763**. The sampled fixed-head gap is about.7769nats and additional learned-mass gap about1.2573nats. Finite head resolution explains some, but not all, of the observed deficiency. These measured gaps are not universal bounds.

Seeds share the same evaluation tasks; conditional intervals average across models within each task before treating task draws as independent units. Training-seed SD and conditional paired-task t intervals are displayed separately. Full x/y/logit arrays, GP moments, seeds, dtype/shape/hash, traces and state hashes are saved. Wall-clock runtime is recorded with variable host load and is not compared with paper hardware.
